# Jesus's Private Teachings to Disciples - Chronological List

## Methodology Note
This catalog focuses on **private instruction** given to the Twelve (and sometimes a smaller inner circle) as distinct from public speeches to crowds. These are intimate teaching moments, commissioning instructions, and post-resurrection appearances.

**Timeline:** ~27-30 CE (Jesus's 3-year ministry) + 40 days post-resurrection

## DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF MAJOR DISCIPLE TEACHINGS

### 1. SENDING OF THE TWELVE (Matthew 10:5-42)
**Date:** Summer 28 CE  
**Context:** First apostolic mission  
**Audience:** The Twelve exclusively

**Instructions Include:**
- **Territory:** Go to lost sheep of Israel, not Gentiles/Samaritans (10:5-6)
- **Message:** "Kingdom of heaven is at hand" (10:7)
- **Authority:** Heal sick, raise dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons (10:8)
- **Provisions:** "Freely received, freely give," No gold/silver/copper (10:8-10)
- **Hospitality:** Find worthy house, stay there (10:11-15)
- **Opposition:** "I send you as sheep among wolves," Be wise as serpents (10:16)
- **Persecution:** Delivered to councils, flogged, before governors/kings (10:17-20)
- **Family Division:** Brother against brother, children vs. parents (10:21-22)
- **Endurance:** "Whoever endures to end will be saved" (10:22)
- **Flee Persecution:** "When persecuted in one town, flee to next" (10:23)
- **Disciple Like Master:** If they called master Beelzebul... (10:24-25)
- **Fear Not (3x):** 
  - "Do not fear them" (10:26)
  - "Do not fear those who kill body" (10:28)
  - "Fear him who can destroy soul and body in hell" (10:28)
- **Sparrows:** Worth more than many sparrows (10:29-31)
- **Confession:** "Whoever acknowledges me..." (10:32-33)
- **Sword Not Peace:** "I came not to bring peace but sword" (10:34-36)
- **Cost:** "Whoever loves father/mother more than me..." (10:37-39)
- **Reward:** "Whoever receives you receives me" (10:40-42)

**Result:** They went out, preached repentance, cast out demons, healed (Mark 6:12-13)

### 2. CAESAREA PHILIPPI: "WHO DO YOU SAY I AM?" (Matthew 16:13-28)
**Date:** Summer 29 CE  
**Context:** Turning point of ministry, withdrawal to north  
**Audience:** The Twelve

**The Exchange:**
- **Jesus:** "Who do people say the Son of Man is?" (16:13)
- **Disciples:** "Some say John the Baptist, others Elijah, Jeremiah, or one of prophets" (16:14)
- **Jesus:** "But who do you say that I am?" (16:15)
- **Peter:** "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God" (16:16)

**Jesus's Response to Peter:**
- "Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah! Flesh and blood has not revealed this, but my Father" (16:17)
- "You are Peter (Petros), on this rock (petra) I will build my church" (16:18)
- "Gates of hell shall not prevail against it" (16:18)
- "I will give you keys of kingdom of heaven" (16:19)
- "Whatever you bind on earth...loose on earth..." (16:19)

**First Passion Prediction:**
- "Must go to Jerusalem, suffer many things from elders/chief priests/scribes" (16:21)
- "Be killed, and on third day be raised" (16:21)

**Peter's Rebuke & Jesus's Response:**
- Peter: "Far be it from you, Lord! This shall never happen" (16:22)
- Jesus: "Get behind me, Satan! You are a hindrance...not setting mind on things of God but man" (16:23)

**Cost of Discipleship:**
- "If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself, take up cross, follow me" (16:24)
- "Whoever would save his life will lose it" (16:25)
- "What will it profit a man to gain whole world and forfeit his soul?" (16:26)
- "Son of Man will come with his angels in glory of Father, repay each for what he has done" (16:27)
- "Some standing here will not taste death until they see Son of Man coming in kingdom" (16:28)

**Significance:** Most important mid-ministry teaching—Peter's confession, passion prediction, cross-bearing

### 3. THE TRANSFIGURATION (Matthew 17:1-13, Mark 9:2-13, Luke 9:28-36)
**Date:** Summer 29 CE, 6-8 days after Caesarea Philippi  
**Location:** "High mountain" (tradition: Mt. Tabor or Mt. Hermon)  
**Audience:** Peter, James, John only (inner circle)

**The Event:**
- Jesus "transfigured before them" (17:2)
- Face shone like sun, clothes white as light (17:2)
- Moses and Elijah appeared, talking with Jesus (17:3)
- Luke: They discussed his "departure" (exodus) in Jerusalem (Luke 9:31)

**Peter's Response:**
- "Lord, it is good that we are here" (17:4)
- "If you wish, I will make three tents" (17:4)
- Mark: "He did not know what to say, they were terrified" (Mark 9:6)

**Father's Voice:**
- Bright cloud overshadowed them (17:5)
- "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased; listen to him" (17:5)
- Disciples fell on faces in terror (17:6)

**Jesus's Instructions:**
- Touched them: "Rise, have no fear" (17:7)
- "Tell no one the vision until Son of Man is raised from the dead" (17:9)

**Elijah Discussion:**
- Disciples: "Why do scribes say Elijah must come first?" (17:10)
- Jesus: "Elijah does come and will restore all things" (17:11)
- "But I tell you Elijah has already come" [John the Baptist] (17:12)
- "They did not recognize him but did to him whatever they pleased" (17:12)

**Significance:** 
- Confirms Jesus's divine sonship
- Moses (Law) and Elijah (Prophets) testify to Jesus
- Prepares inner circle for crucifixion
- Glory glimpsed before suffering

### 4. UPPER ROOM DISCOURSE (John 13-17)
**Date:** Thursday evening, April 2, 30 CE  
**Context:** Last Supper, final hours before arrest  
**Audience:** The Eleven (Judas left after bread)  
**Duration:** Approximately 2-3 hours

**Structure - Five Major Sections:**

#### A. FOOT WASHING & BETRAYAL (John 13)
- Washed disciples' feet (13:1-11)
- "Do you understand what I have done?" (13:12)
- "You call me Teacher and Lord, and you are right" (13:13)
- "If I then...have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet" (13:14)
- "I have given you an example" (13:15)
- "No servant greater than master" (13:16)
- Betrayer predicted, Judas identified, receives bread, goes out (13:18-30)
- **New Commandment:** "Love one another as I have loved you" (13:34)
- "By this all will know you are my disciples, if you have love" (13:35)
- Peter's denial predicted (13:36-38)

#### B. THE WAY, TRUTH, LIFE (John 14)
- "Let not your hearts be troubled" (14:1)
- "I go to prepare a place for you" (14:2)
- "I will come again and will take you to myself" (14:3)
- Thomas: "We do not know where you are going" (14:5)
- **"I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to Father except through me"** (14:6)
- Philip: "Show us the Father" (14:8)
- "Whoever has seen me has seen the Father" (14:9)
- "I am in the Father and the Father is in me" (14:10)
- "Greater works than these will you do" (14:12)
- "Whatever you ask in my name, this I will do" (14:13-14)
- **Promise of Holy Spirit:**
  - "I will ask Father, he will give you another Helper/Advocate" (14:16)
  - "Spirit of truth, whom world cannot receive" (14:17)
  - "I will not leave you as orphans; I will come to you" (14:18)
  - "He will teach you all things, bring to remembrance all I said" (14:26)
- **Peace:** "Peace I leave with you, my peace I give to you" (14:27)
- "Not as world gives do I give to you" (14:27)
- "Let not hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid" (14:27)
- "I am going away, and I will come to you" (14:28)
- "I have told you before it takes place, so when it does you may believe" (14:29)

#### C. THE VINE AND BRANCHES (John 15:1-17)
- **"I am the true vine, and my Father is the vinedresser"** (15:1)
- "Every branch that does not bear fruit he takes away" (15:2)
- "Every branch that bears fruit he prunes, that it may bear more" (15:2)
- **"Abide in me, and I in you"** (15:4)
- "As branch cannot bear fruit by itself unless it abides in vine, neither can you unless abide in me" (15:4)
- **"I am the vine; you are the branches"** (15:5)
- "Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit" (15:5)
- **"Apart from me you can do nothing"** (15:5)
- "If anyone does not abide in me he is thrown away like branch, withers" (15:6)
- "If you abide in me, and my words abide in you, ask whatever you wish, it will be done" (15:7)
- "By this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit" (15:8)
- "As Father has loved me, so have I loved you. Abide in my love" (15:9)
- "If you keep my commandments, you will abide in my love" (15:10)
- "These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and your joy may be full" (15:11)
- **"This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you"** (15:12)
- "Greater love has no one than this, that someone lay down his life for his friends" (15:13)
- **"You are my friends if you do what I command you"** (15:14)
- "No longer do I call you servants...I have called you friends" (15:15)
- "You did not choose me, but I chose you" (15:16)
- "I appointed you that you should go and bear fruit, and that your fruit should abide" (15:16)

#### D. WORLD'S HATRED & SPIRIT'S TESTIMONY (John 15:18-16:15)
- "If the world hates you, know that it has hated me before it hated you" (15:18)
- "If you were of the world, the world would love you as its own" (15:19)
- "I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you" (15:19)
- **"A servant is not greater than his master"** (15:20)
- "If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you" (15:20)
- "They will do all these things to you on account of my name" (15:21)
- "Whoever hates me hates my Father also" (15:23)
- "They have seen and hated both me and my Father" (15:24)
- **Spirit's Work:**
  - "When the Helper comes, whom I will send from Father, Spirit of truth who proceeds from Father, he will bear witness about me" (15:26)
  - "You also will bear witness" (15:27)
- "They will put you out of synagogues" (16:2)
- "Hour is coming when whoever kills you will think he is offering service to God" (16:2)
- "It is to your advantage that I go away" (16:7)
- "If I do not go, Helper will not come to you" (16:7)
- **"When he comes, he will convict the world concerning:**
  - **Sin** - because they do not believe in me (16:9)
  - **Righteousness** - because I go to Father (16:10)
  - **Judgment** - because ruler of this world is judged (16:11)
- "When Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all the truth" (16:13)
- "He will glorify me" (16:14)

#### E. SORROW TO JOY & OVERCOMING WORLD (John 16:16-33)
- "A little while, and you will see me no longer; again a little while, you will see me" (16:16)
- Disciples confused, questioning among themselves (16:17-18)
- **Woman in Labor Analogy:**
  - "When woman is giving birth she has sorrow...but when she delivers baby she no longer remembers anguish, for joy that human being has been born" (16:21)
  - "So also you have sorrow now, but I will see you again, your hearts will rejoice" (16:22)
- "In that day you will ask nothing of me" (16:23)
- "Whatever you ask of Father in my name, he will give it to you" (16:23)
- "Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be full" (16:24)
- "I have said these things in figures of speech. Hour is coming when I will no longer speak in figures but tell you plainly about the Father" (16:25)
- "In that day you will ask in my name" (16:26)
- "Father himself loves you, because you have loved me and believed I came from God" (16:27)
- **Jesus's Declaration:**
  - "I came from the Father and have come into the world" (16:28)
  - "I am leaving the world and going to the Father" (16:28)
- Disciples: "Now you are speaking plainly!" (16:29)
- "Now we know that you know all things...by this we believe you came from God" (16:30)
- Jesus: "Do you now believe?" (16:31)
- "Hour is coming, indeed has come, when you will be scattered, each to his own home, and will leave me alone" (16:32)
- "Yet I am not alone, for the Father is with me" (16:32)
- **Final Word:** **"I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world."** (16:33)

#### F. HIGH PRIESTLY PRAYER (John 17) - Jesus Prays for Disciples
**Structure:** Jesus prays for (1) Himself, (2) His disciples, (3) All future believers

**For Himself (17:1-5):**
- "Father, the hour has come; glorify your Son that Son may glorify you" (17:1)
- "I glorified you on earth, having accomplished the work you gave me" (17:4)
- "Glorify me in your own presence with glory I had with you before world existed" (17:5)

**For His Disciples (17:6-19):**
- "I have manifested your name to the people you gave me out of the world" (17:6)
- "They were yours, you gave them to me, and they have kept your word" (17:6)
- "Now they know that everything you have given me is from you" (17:7)
- **"I am praying for them. I am not praying for the world but for those you have given me"** (17:9)
- "I am no longer in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to you" (17:11)
- **"Holy Father, keep them in your name, which you have given me, that they may be one, even as we are one"** (17:11)
- "While I was with them, I kept them in your name...I guarded them, and not one of them has been lost except the son of destruction" (17:12)
- "I do not ask that you take them out of the world, but that you keep them from the evil one" (17:15)
- "They are not of the world, just as I am not of the world" (17:16)
- **"Sanctify them in the truth; your word is truth"** (17:17)
- "As you sent me into the world, so I have sent them into the world" (17:18)
- "For their sake I consecrate myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth" (17:19)

**For All Believers (17:20-26):**
- **"I do not ask for these only, but also for those who will believe in me through their word"** (17:20)
- **"That they may all be one, just as you, Father, are in me, and I in you, that they also may be in us"** (17:21)
- "So that the world may believe that you have sent me" (17:21)
- "The glory that you have given me I have given to them, that they may be one even as we are one" (17:22)
- "I in them and you in me, that they may become perfectly one" (17:23)
- "So that the world may know that you sent me and loved them even as you loved me" (17:23)
- "Father, I desire that they also, whom you have given me, may be with me where I am, to see my glory" (17:24)
- "I made known to them your name, and I will continue to make it known, that the love with which you have loved me may be in them, and I in them" (17:26)

**Significance of Upper Room Discourse:**
- Longest continuous teaching session recorded
- Most intimate, tender moment with disciples
- Final instructions before death
- Promise of Holy Spirit's coming
- Theme of love, unity, abiding
- Prayer reveals Jesus's heart for his followers

### 5. GREAT COMMISSION (Matthew 28:16-20)
**Date:** Late April 30 CE, during 40-day post-resurrection period  
**Location:** Mountain in Galilee (Jesus appointed)  
**Audience:** The Eleven

**The Declaration:**
- Some worshiped, but some doubted (28:17)
- Jesus came and said to them: (28:18-20)

**"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me."** (28:18)

**"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations,"** (28:19a)

**"baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit,"** (28:19b)

**"teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you."** (28:20a)

**"And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age."** (28:20b)

**Parallel Commissions:**
- **Mark 16:15-18:** "Go into all the world, proclaim gospel to whole creation, whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, signs will accompany"
- **Luke 24:46-49:** "Repentance for forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem, I am sending promise of my Father, stay in city until clothed with power from on high"
- **John 20:21-23:** "As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you...Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive sins of any, they are forgiven"
- **Acts 1:8:** "You will receive power when Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth"

**Significance:**
- Final marching orders for church
- Universal scope: "all nations"
- Trinitarian baptismal formula
- Promise of Jesus's presence forever
- Foundation for missionary movement

### 6. POST-RESURRECTION APPEARANCES & TEACHINGS

#### Resurrection Sunday (April 5, 30 CE)
1. **To Mary Magdalene** (John 20:11-18) - Garden tomb
   - "Do not cling to me, for I have not yet ascended"
   - "Go to my brothers and say to them, 'I am ascending to my Father and your Father, to my God and your God'"

2. **To Two on Road to Emmaus** (Luke 24:13-35)
   - Explained Moses and all Prophets concerning himself
   - Recognized in breaking of bread

3. **To the Eleven** (Luke 24:36-49, John 20:19-23) - Upper room, evening
   - "Peace be with you"
   - Showed hands and side
   - "As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you"
   - Breathed on them: "Receive the Holy Spirit"
   - "If you forgive sins...if you withhold forgiveness..."

#### One Week Later (April 12, 30 CE)
4. **Thomas Present** (John 20:24-29) - Upper room
   - "Put your finger here, and see my hands. Put out your hand, and place it in my side"
   - Thomas: "My Lord and my God!"
   - "Have you believed because you have seen me? Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed"

#### Back in Galilee (Late April 30 CE)
5. **Sea of Galilee Fishing** (John 21:1-14)
   - Seven disciples fishing all night, caught nothing
   - Jesus on shore: "Cast net on right side"
   - 153 large fish
   - "Come and have breakfast"

6. **Restoration of Peter** (John 21:15-23)
   - Three times: "Simon, son of John, do you love me?"
   - Three times: "Feed my lambs/sheep"
   - Predicted Peter's death: "When you are old, you will stretch out your hands"
   - "Follow me"
   - Concerning John: "If it is my will that he remain until I come, what is that to you?"

#### 40 Days of Teaching (April-May 30 CE)
7. **Kingdom of God Teaching** (Acts 1:3)
   - "He presented himself alive after his suffering by many proofs"
   - "Appearing to them during forty days"
   - "Speaking about the kingdom of God"

8. **Wait for Promise** (Acts 1:4-8)
   - "Do not depart from Jerusalem, but wait for the promise of the Father"
   - "John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit not many days from now"
   - "You will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you"
   - "You will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, Judea, Samaria, to end of earth"

#### The Ascension (May 14, 30 CE)
9. **Final Blessing & Departure** (Luke 24:50-53, Acts 1:9-11) - Mount of Olives
   - Led them out to Bethany
   - Lifted up his hands and blessed them
   - While blessing, parted from them, carried up into heaven

   - Two men in white: "This Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come in the same way as you saw him go into heaven"

## NOTABLE PATTERNS

### Jesus's Teaching Methods with Disciples:
1. **Explanation:** Interprets parables privately
2. **Example:** Foot washing, servant leadership
3. **Experience:** Sends them out on missions
4. **Correction:** Rebukes misunderstanding (Peter's "Get behind me, Satan")
5. **Prediction:** Prepares them for what's coming (passion, persecution)
6. **Prayer:** High Priestly Prayer models intercession
7. **Repetition:** Three passion predictions, three denials predicted
8. **Question & Answer:** "Who do you say I am?" "Do you love me?"

### Progression of Revelation:
- **Identity:** From "Rabbi" → "Christ" → "Son of God" → "My Lord and my God"
- **Mission:** From "lost sheep of Israel" → "all nations"
- **Understanding:** From confusion → clarity after resurrection
- **Empowerment:** From "apart from me you can do nothing" → "greater works than these"
