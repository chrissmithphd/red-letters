# Jesus's Public Speeches - Chronological List

## Methodology Note
This chronology follows the general scholarly consensus based on:
- Gospel narrative sequence (primarily harmonizing Matthew, Mark, Luke)
- John's chronology (which mentions specific feasts)
- Historical context (Passover timing, feast days)
- Jesus's ministry timeline: ~27-30 CE (approximately 3 years)

**Caveat:** Exact chronology is debated among scholars. This represents the most commonly accepted order.

## DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF MAJOR PUBLIC DISCOURSES

### 1. SERMON ON THE MOUNT (Matthew 5-7)
**Date:** Spring 28 CE, early in Galilean ministry  
**Duration:** Estimated 20-30 minutes (reading time)  
**Setting:** Mountain/hillside near Capernaum, large crowds

**Structure:**
- **Introduction:** Beatitudes (5:3-12)
- **Identity:** Salt and Light (5:13-16)
- **Law:** Fulfillment not abolition (5:17-20)
- **Antitheses:** Six contrasts "You have heard...but I say" (5:21-48)
  - Murder → Anger
  - Adultery → Lust
  - Divorce
  - Oaths
  - Eye for eye → Turn other cheek
  - Love neighbor → Love enemies
- **Piety:** Giving, Prayer (Lord's Prayer), Fasting (6:1-18)
- **Priorities:** Treasures in heaven, Single eye, God vs. Money (6:19-24)
- **Worry:** Do not worry about life (6:25-34)
- **Relationships:** Judging others (7:1-6)
- **Prayer:** Ask, seek, knock; Golden Rule (7:7-12)
- **Choice:** Narrow gate vs. wide gate (7:13-14)
- **Discernment:** False prophets, tree by fruit (7:15-23)
- **Conclusion:** Wise vs. foolish builder (7:24-27)

**Reaction:** "Crowds were astonished at his teaching, for he taught as one having authority" (7:28-29)

---

### 2. SERMON ON THE PLAIN (Luke 6:17-49)
**Date:** Spring 28 CE (possibly same occasion as Sermon on Mount, Luke's shorter version)  
**Duration:** Estimated 10-15 minutes  
**Setting:** "Level place" after choosing 12 apostles

**Structure:**
- **Beatitudes:** Four blessings (6:20-23)
- **Woes:** Four woes to rich/full/laughing/well-spoken-of (6:24-26)
- **Love Enemies:** Extended teaching (6:27-36)
- **Judging:** Speck vs. log (6:37-42)
- **Tree and Fruit:** Good/bad fruit (6:43-45)
- **Foundation:** Building on rock vs. sand (6:46-49)

---

### 3. PARABLES BY THE SEA (Matthew 13, Mark 4, Luke 8)
**Date:** Summer 28 CE  
**Setting:** Taught from boat due to large crowd

**Parables Told:**
1. **Sower/Soils** (Matt 13:3-9, 18-23) - Reception of the word
2. **Weeds/Tares** (Matt 13:24-30, 36-43) - Kingdom & judgment
3. **Mustard Seed** (Matt 13:31-32) - Kingdom growth
4. **Leaven** (Matt 13:33) - Kingdom permeation
5. **Hidden Treasure** (Matt 13:44) - Kingdom worth
6. **Pearl** (Matt 13:45-46) - Kingdom worth
7. **Net** (Matt 13:47-50) - Final judgment
8. **Growing Seed** (Mark 4:26-29) - Kingdom mystery
9. **Lamp** (Mark 4:21-25) - Revelation

**Theme:** Kingdom of Heaven revealed through parables

---

### 4. BREAD OF LIFE DISCOURSE (John 6:22-71)
**Date:** Spring 29 CE, day after feeding 5,000  
**Setting:** Capernaum synagogue  
**Catalyst:** Crowd seeks Jesus after miracle

**Key Statements:**
- "Do not work for food that perishes" (6:27)
- "I am the bread of life" (6:35, 48)
- "Whoever comes to me shall not hunger" (6:35)
- "I am the living bread that came down from heaven" (6:51)
- "Unless you eat the flesh of the Son of Man and drink his blood, you have no life in you" (6:53)

**Result:** Many disciples turned back (6:66)

---

### 5. FEAST OF TABERNACLES TEACHING (John 7-8)
**Date:** Fall 29 CE (September/October), mid-ministry  
**Setting:** Jerusalem Temple during 7-day harvest festival

**Key Themes:**
- Identity of Jesus (7:14-36)
- Rivers of living water / Holy Spirit (7:37-39)
- Light of the world (8:12)
- True witness (8:13-20)
- Where I am going (8:21-30)
- Truth sets free (8:31-38)
- Children of Abraham vs. children of devil (8:39-47)
- "Before Abraham was, I AM" (8:58)

**Controversy:** Claims of divinity, attempted stoning

---

### 6. OLIVET DISCOURSE (Matthew 24-25, Mark 13, Luke 21)
**Date:** Tuesday evening, March 31, 30 CE (3 days before crucifixion)  
**Setting:** Mount of Olives, overlooking Temple  
**Audience:** Peter, James, John, Andrew ask privately (but recorded for all)

**Structure:**
- **Temple Destruction:** "Not one stone left on another" (24:2)
- **Signs:** Wars, famines, earthquakes, persecution (24:3-14)
- **Great Tribulation:** Abomination of desolation (24:15-28)
- **Second Coming:** Son of Man on clouds (24:29-31)
- **Fig Tree Lesson:** Know season (24:32-35)
- **Day Unknown:** "No one knows the day or hour" (24:36-44)
- **Faithful Servant:** Wise vs. wicked servant (24:45-51)
- **Ten Virgins:** Readiness parable (25:1-13)
- **Talents:** Stewardship parable (25:14-30)
- **Sheep & Goats:** Final judgment scene (25:31-46)

**Significance:** Jesus's most comprehensive teaching on eschatology

---

### 7. WOES AGAINST SCRIBES & PHARISEES (Matthew 23)
**Date:** Tuesday, March 31, 30 CE (3 days before crucifixion)  
**Setting:** Temple courts, very public

**The Seven Woes:**
1. Shut kingdom of heaven (23:13)
2. Make converts twice as bad (23:15)
3. Blind guides about oaths (23:16-22)
4. Tithe mint/dill/cumin but neglect justice/mercy/faith (23:23-24)
5. Clean outside of cup but inside full of greed (23:25-26)
6. Whitewashed tombs (23:27-28)
7. Build tombs of prophets but kill prophets (23:29-36)

**Conclusion:** "O Jerusalem, Jerusalem...how often would I have gathered your children" (23:37-39)

## SUMMARY STATISTICS

### By Ministry Year:
- **Year 1 (28 CE):** 5 major public speeches
- **Year 2 (29 CE):** 9 major teaching events
- **Year 3 (30 CE):** 6 major speeches (concentrated in final week)

### By Type:
- **Synagogue Sermons:** 3 (Nazareth, Capernaum, Bread of Life)
- **Mountain/Outdoor Sermons:** 3 (Sermon on Mount, Sermon on Plain, Olivet)
- **Temple Teaching:** 7 (mostly final week)
- **Parables Collections:** 2 major sessions
- **Feast Day Discourses:** 2 (Tabernacles, Dedication)

### By Location:
- **Galilee region:** 8 speeches
- **Jerusalem/Temple:** 8 speeches
- **Journey teachings:** 4 major collections

### Longest Discourses:
1. **Sermon on the Mount** - 3 chapters (Matthew 5-7)
2. **Olivet Discourse** - 2 chapters (Matthew 24-25)
3. **Journey to Jerusalem** - 9 chapters (Luke 9:51-18:34)
4. **Feast of Tabernacles** - 2 chapters (John 7-8)

## NOTABLE THEMES ACROSS PUBLIC SPEECHES

### Kingdom of Heaven/God
- Nature of the Kingdom (Parables)
- Entrance requirements (Sermon on Mount)
- Growth and culmination (Olivet Discourse)

### Ethics and Righteousness
- Higher righteousness than Pharisees (Sermon on Mount)
- Love of enemies (Multiple occasions)
- Hypocrisy condemned (Woes)

### Identity of Jesus
- Authority over Law (Sermon on Mount)
- Bread of Life (John 6)
- Light of the World (John 8)
- "I AM" statements (John)
- Messianic claims (Jerusalem)

### Judgment and Eschatology
- Olivet Discourse (detailed)
- Parable of Sheep & Goats
- Final separation (Wheat/Tares)

### Religious Hypocrisy
- Woes against Pharisees (Matthew 23)
- Clean outside/unclean inside (Multiple)
- Traditions vs. Commandments

## SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

**Primary Sources:**
- Matthew (most comprehensive sermon records)
- Mark (earliest gospel, shorter accounts)
- Luke (additional material, parables)
- John (feast discourses, theological depth)

**Chronological Framework:**
- John's gospel provides feast markers (Passover, Tabernacles, Dedication)
- Synoptic sequence (Matthew, Mark, Luke general agreement)
- Passion week timing (all four gospels converge)

**Scholarly Consensus:**
- Jesus's ministry: ~3 years (27-30 CE)
- Three Passovers mentioned in John (2:13, 6:4, 13:1)
- Crucifixion: April 3, 30 CE (or April 7, 30 CE - debated)

## NOTES

1. **"Public" defined as:** Speeches delivered to crowds/multitudes, not private disciple instruction (though Upper Room Discourse in John 13-17 is major, it's private)

2. **Some scholars debate:** Whether Sermon on Mount and Sermon on Plain are same event (Luke's shorter version) or separate occasions with similar content

3. **Journey to Jerusalem** (Luke 9:51-18:34) is a collection of teachings over several months, not one speech

4. **Dating precision:** Exact dates for Year 1-2 events are approximate; Passion Week dates are more certain

5. **Omitted:** Smaller crowd interactions and brief public statements (many in gospels)
